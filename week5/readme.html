It Will Bloom in Time

My vision was a flower. The stems around the flower would spin based on seconds, minutes, and miliseconds. The stem would grow based on days in a week. The grass would grow based on days in a month. And the sun would cross the top of the canvas every hour. It was ambitious and most of my time was spent researching how to incorporate the minute function without breaking the code. I still think the concept is pretty.
