Blinking Cross

I iterated on my sketch from week 3. In adding it to our template, it went from a light pulsing to something sort of like a blinking pulse. It looked cool so I decided to keep it. I wanted to make the colors changed when the user presses with their mouse. Specifically, I wanted holding down to change the color and letting go to change it back. To do that I had to give my previously non filled shapes a fill.
